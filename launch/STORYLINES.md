# Storylines

**Built: A. The calendar rebuilds** (recommended: a stranger follows one calendar and eleven pins, and every number is real)
One frame that rebuilds. The calendar is home; the eleven libraries wait in a rack and drop onto the days they picked for four real phrases, then fly into the leaderboard. Silent; with music it would be a dry 100 bpm pulse, a pop on each landing, the bed opening at the leaderboard.

**B. Type carries the argument.** Cut on the beat: giant serif phrases ("next Friday", "Room 1104", "Sun Microsystems") fill the frame one per half bar, each answered by a dither field of eleven dates, ending on 137.

**C. A moment in someone's day.** A calendar invite says "Lunch next Friday"; two colleagues show up a week apart. Rewind: the same phrase through eleven parsers. Needs a person or a mock chat, so it was the weakest fit for a benchmark.
